## Threat sketch

*Assets* — memory safety and availability of the host process; integrity of the decoded PCM / encoded bitstream
*Adversaries* — a malicious media supplier (controls every decoder byte and the container's AudioSpecificConfig); a malicious PCM supplier (any f32, channel count, rate); a compromised dependency (dev-only closure)
*Highest-value attack path* — a crafted stream driving a SIMD kernel out of bounds (memory corruption); next, a crafted config, extension payload or PCM that panics the codec or forces an oversized output (DoS / integrity)
*Full model* — [`docs/threat-model.md`](../threat-model.md)
