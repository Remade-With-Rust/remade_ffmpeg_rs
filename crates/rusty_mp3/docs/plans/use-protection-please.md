## Threat sketch

*Assets* — memory safety and availability of the host process; integrity of the decoded PCM / encoded bitstream
*Adversaries* — a malicious media supplier (controls every decoder byte); a malicious PCM supplier (any f32, channel count, rate); a compromised dependency (dev-only closure)
*Highest-value attack path* — a crafted stream driving a SIMD kernel out of bounds (memory corruption); next, a crafted stream or PCM that panics the codec (DoS)
*Full model* — [`docs/threat-model.md`](../threat-model.md)
